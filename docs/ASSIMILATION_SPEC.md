# Station quality control, v2

Call `quality_control` with actual UTC measurement times, backgrounds from already-issued forecasts, and positive total and background uncertainty in native units. For hourly truth, measurement time is `valid_time + X_offset_min`. Supply the official tier explicitly. The driver processes simultaneous rows together, preserves input order and returns flags and weights alongside the raw, read-only observations. Each station-variable timestamp must be unique.

| Gate | Bits | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Static | 1 | Check physical bounds, dewpoint against simultaneous temperature, gust against speed, and station pressure against elevation. |
| Temporal | 2, plus 64 for flatline | Check the configured step (temperature defaults to 10 K per 15 minutes) and six consecutive identical values. Zero precipitation is exempt. For wind speed, gust and components, see the repeat rule below. |
| Spatial | 4 for an outlier, 16 for insufficient coverage | Use distinct, eligible non-self neighbours within 25 km. Require three and at least one official or one from a different tenth-degree cell. Count each station once for eligibility. For the median and MAD, an official value has weight 2 and a citizen value weight 1. Compare at four robust standard deviations with the configured scale floor. Adjust temperature and dewpoint by 6.5 K/km and station pressure to target elevation using the configured scale height. |
| Background | 8 | Standardize the innovation after the official background correction and the prior station day/night bias. Huber weight is 1 through 2.5 sigma and 2.5 divided by absolute z thereafter. Beyond 8 sigma require two agreeing buddies above 3 sigma, including an agreeing official or a citizen in a different cell. |
| Copycat | 32 | Compare against an independently identified reference. A seven-day correlation above 0.998 with near-zero RMS difference gives zero weight. |

**Wind repeats.** At or below 0.5 m/s in absolute value, a repeated wind reading counts as calm. A non-calm constant run must span at least 75 minutes with no gap over 15 minutes and contain at least six identical reports. A simultaneous or recently measured comparison within half a 0.5 m/s reporting bin supports a legitimate repeat. Without that corroboration the v1 six-report frozen-anemometer case at 15-minute spacing still flags. A changing signal resets the run.

**Thermal background correction.** For each official site keep the sum and count of official observation minus GEFS by UTC measurement hour. Use only strictly earlier measurements when correcting a citizen's temperature or dewpoint innovation. The batch driver takes the nearest available same-hour history within 150 km. The trial uses the geographically nearest official station within that radius and skips the thermal background decision where it lacks past same-hour evidence. Its two-day warm-up uses July forecasts and official measurements. The API also accepts an independently prepared causal `official_background_bias` array. Day means solar elevation above 10°. A missing correction supplies no evidence for a thermal bias update.

**Temperature credential.** The verified tier applies to air temperature. Wind, humidity and other channels retain their own priors. At the first temperature report of a UTC day, examine the preceding seven completed UTC days. Each day needs at least six distinct independently measured official comparisons, with at least 84 in total. The trial requires the nearest official site within 25 km and a measurement no more than 15 minutes old. Pass the actual independent measurement times in `reference_time_ns` to earn a tier through the public API. Reuse of the same measurement adds no verification credit. Accepted, non-copycat, non-hard-failed reports with positive Huber weight and a defined rolling reference correlation contribute to the daily signed temperature error.

Every daily mean citizen-minus-official error must lie within one background sigma of zero, and the range of those seven means must fit within one sigma. A low buddy or official temperature correlation (below 0.5), or a copycat flag, disqualifies that day's evidence and withdraws the credential. Copycat evidence on any channel also vetoes an earned tier for seven days. A missing verification day breaks the streak. These are operational eligibility rules for this trial, without independent calibration of their false-positive or false-negative rates.

**Final weight.** Multiply the tier prior by the Huber weight, background reliability, solar penalty and correlation guard, then clip to [0, 1]. Priors are 1 for official or verified temperature and 0.5 for new citizens. A hard failure or copycat receives zero. Reliability is `exp(-sqrt(mse_ema) / sigma_bg)`. Daytime temperature and dewpoint use `exp(-max(0, abs(bias_day - bias_night) - 1) / 2)` once both corrected EMAs exist. The correlation guard decays by 0.9 per update below 0.5 correlation with the buddy median. The latest final weight is the station-variable QoD score.

Serialize each `StationState` using `dataclasses.asdict` and JSON, then restore with `StationState(**record)`. Verification days, official hourly accumulators and wind-run start times survive replay. V1 state lacking the new fields begins with no v2 verification credit. Thresholds and v1 values are named in `QCConfig`. The CPU QC path has synthetic regression coverage and an aggregate real-data trial. HealDA and SDA integration remain deferred.
